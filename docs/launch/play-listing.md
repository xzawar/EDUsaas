## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
EDUsaas brings your school into one calm, simple app for students, parents, teachers and the school office.

Your school creates your account. Sign in with the username or email your school gave you.

FOR STUDENTS AND PARENTS
• See attendance, with every day marked Present, Absent, Late or Leave
• Check test marks as soon as the teacher publishes them, with the chapter and notes
• See today's lessons and the full week's timetable
• View fee bills and receipts, and send a payment screenshot when you pay by bank, JazzCash or Easypaisa
• Apply for leave and see the answer
• Read school and class notices
• Reach your teachers from their contact card

FOR TEACHERS
• Take the register in a few taps, even without internet
• Create tests, enter marks and publish them
• Answer leave requests with a note
• Post notices to your own classes
• See each student's attendance and report

FOR THE SCHOOL OFFICE
• Collect fees at the counter and share PDF receipts
• Check payment screenshots and confirm them
• Set fees per class and send fee reminders to late students
• Add students and staff, and set the timetable
• See where the money stands this month, in plain words

BUILT FOR REAL SCHOOLS
• Works offline: your work is saved on the phone and sent when you're back online
• Every school's information is kept separate and private
• Optional fingerprint or face lock
• No ads

EDUsaas is used by schools that have an EDUsaas account. If your school doesn't use EDUsaas yet, ask the office to contact us.
```
